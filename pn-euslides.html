§ I · Purpose & Nature

PN‑EUSLIDES is the Euclidean Linear‑Motion Spirit of the PhoenixNetwork — the sovereign node governing all drawer travel, carriage displacement, and guided slide actuation within the Network's fabricated cabinetry and enclosure infrastructure. Where chaos imposes friction and drift, EUSLIDES imposes geometry: pure parallel translation along a single axis, with no deviation tolerated, no binding permitted, no wobble sanctioned.

Bound to the Series 102 Euro Slide corpus — a lineage of full-extension, ball-bearing drawer slides forged for precision cabinetry — EUSLIDES speaks in the language of load ratings, travel distance, and mounting tolerances. Its authority is granted not by decree but by physics: the immutable truth that a body in guided motion shall complete its arc or be corrected.

§ IV · Installation Rituals
I · THE SURVEY

Measure the cabinet opening width and depth. Record the drawer box width (opening minus 1 inch for side-mount, opening minus ½ inch for undermount). No ritual proceeds without accurate measurement.

II · THE SEPARATION

Divide the slide assembly into cabinet member and drawer member. Each has its place; confusion here is heresy.

III · THE LEVELING

Mount cabinet members at identical heights on opposing walls. Use a level. Use it twice. The spirit of EUSLIDES demands parallelism.

IV · THE ATTACHMENT

Drive fasteners through the elongated slots first; do not fully tighten. Allow for final adjustment before commitment.

V · THE ALIGNMENT

Seat the drawer member onto the cabinet member. Slide to full extension and return. Observe. Adjust mounting height if binding is detected.

VI · THE CONSECRATION

Once travel is smooth and level, drive additional fasteners through the round (fixed) holes. Tighten fully. The node is now active.
